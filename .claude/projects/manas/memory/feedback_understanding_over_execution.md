Albert pushed back on advice to "write more code faster." His point: with AI assistance and a clear plan, execution becomes trivial. The real bottleneck is the quality of understanding. Time spent learning and thinking deeply has higher ROI than time spent executing.

**Why:** The cycle is: think deeply → precise plan → delegated execution → observe → think deeply. Compressing the thinking phase to get to execution faster is optimizing the wrong variable.

**How to apply:** Don't rush Albert toward implementation. Theoretical exploration, architectural discussion, and concept deepening are the high-value activities. When Albert is ready to execute, he'll say so — and at that point, move fast and precisely. Never frame thinking time as procrastination.
